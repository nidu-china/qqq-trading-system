  Attempt 1: Remove 2-bar MACDf accel (statistics show only 20% of UP swings
             have it). Result: -$4,701 vs baseline.
    Why it failed: The 2-bar accel acts as a TIMING quality gate, not a
    direction filter. Without it, OR reversion CALL fires repeatedly in the
    09:40-10:00 window generating small but frequent losses.

  Attempt 2: 1-bar accel + band_pos -0.65. Result: -$1,912 vs baseline.
    Why it failed: 1-bar accel fires earlier/prematurely. The original
    Jul-08 +$497 OR reversion CALL was replaced by -$210 and -$287 entries
    that fired 3 minutes too early, using up the cooldown window.

  Attempt 3: Extend entry window to 12:30 (OR reversion only). Result: +$50.
    Why it failed: 0DTE Theta decay accelerates after 12:00. The extended
    trades average -$13/trade. For options, the price movement does not
    translate to proportional option gains in the afternoon.

  Attempt 4: MACD direction filter for Momentum (MACDf < 0 for CALL, > 0 for
             PUT). Result: -$1,324 vs baseline.
    Why it failed: Two of the strategy's largest winning sessions (Jul-17
    CALL +$2,420 and Aug-03 CALL +$1,600) had MACDf turning positive during
    the reversal, so the filter blocked them entirely.
